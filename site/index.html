How it behaves

  • Sits under every window. Clicks go straight through.
  • Can draw over your own desktop picture.
  • Cycles scenes when you’re away, and snaps back when you return. 90 seconds in the app, 10 here.
  • ⌘⇧3 can’t see it, so ⌘⌥S and ⌘⌥E capture it.
  • Quit, and your old wallpaper comes back.

Put it on your Mac

  1. Download ASCII-Arcade.dmg and drag it to Applications.

  2. It’s unsigned, so the first time, Control-click it and choose Open. Or in Terminal:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/ASCII Arcade.app"
  3. Find ◎ in your menu bar.

Not on a Mac

cargo run -p aa -- play donut
