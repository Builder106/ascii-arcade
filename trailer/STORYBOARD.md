# Tier 3 trailer storyboard

Built in HyperFrames. `AsciiArcadeTrailer` is 39.5 seconds at 1920×1080,
30 fps. The music starts at 2.51 s so Pixelland's first beat hits at 3.0 s,
right where the boot log dissolves into the cabinet. Every cut after that is
on a beat.

| Time | Beat | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.00-7.01 | Power-on, boot log, the cabinet pulls out of the glass | `attract` take, 0.00 s |
| 7.01-13.79 | The wall as a Mac wallpaper under two windows; the ◎ menu switches Matrix to Life | `wall` take, 8.90 s, under `compositions/desk.html` |
| 13.79-20.04 | Five scenes in four themes, a cut every two beats | `wall` take, six shots, labels from `compositions/montage.html` |
| 20.04-21.62 | Play DOOM here: the camera goes through the glass | `doom` take, 2.62 s |
| 21.62-27.36 | Into a new game | `doom` take, 7.00 s |
| 27.36-33.62 | Idle at the coin door; the loop swings back to the title, the mouse snaps it back | `player` take, 31.83 s |
| 33.62-39.50 | End card beside the coin door | `attract` take, 55.00 s, under `compositions/end.html` |

Captions are in `compositions/captions.html`. Over the site's own stations
they stay in the empty top left so they don't land on the page's copy.

Sound: `audio.mp3` from 2.51 s, fading out over the last 1.4 s. On top of
that, the power-on at the start, blips for the menu, whooshes for the camera
moves, and a coin at the end. Credit the music the way `public/CREDITS.md`
says when you publish.
